These Terms of Service ("Terms") govern use of the Service by any school, member of school staff, or other organisation that creates an account ("you", "the School").

If you don't have authority to agree to these Terms on the School's behalf, please ask a colleague who does (for example, the School's headteacher or business manager) to review and accept them.

Our Privacy Policy and (once signed) our Data Processing Agreement form part of these Terms and explain how personal data is handled.

2. The Service

Emeejay Assess lets school staff record and track pupil assessment results across phonics, maths, times tables, English, and other subjects; run live in-class or remote assessment sessions; view analytics and gap analysis; set targets; and generate parent-ready progress reports.

The content library – questions, checks, and topics – is continually expanding and improving. We don't guarantee that every topic, tier, or question is available or fully polished at any given time; staff can flag anything that looks wrong using the in-app "Report this question" links.

3. Accounts

  • Accounts are created using a school email address on the School's own domain. We do this so that only genuine staff at a school can create or join that school's account, and so that each school domain can only start one free trial.
  • You're responsible for keeping login credentials confidential and for all activity that happens under your account. Tell us straight away if you think an account has been compromised.
  • The School's designated "owner" account can manage which staff have access, and can grant or remove permission for other staff to edit or delete data (see the in-app Staff screen).

4. Free trial

New schools can try the Service free for 14 days. To start a trial, the School's account owner adds a payment card via our payment provider, Stripe, when creating the school account – no payment is taken at this point. If the card step isn't completed, the account stays inactive and can't be used until it is.

The trial automatically converts into a paid annual subscription at the end of the 14 days, and the card on file is charged the then-current annual fee, unless the School cancels before the trial ends (see section 5).

5. Fees and payment

  • The Service is billed as a single annual fee per school (currently £75/year), covering unlimited staff logins, unlimited children, and unlimited assessments across every subject – the current price is always shown before checkout.
  • Payment is processed by our payment provider, Stripe. We never see or store full card details.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically each year on the anniversary of the trial starting, and the card on file is charged automatically, unless cancelled before the renewal date.
  • A School's account owner can cancel or manage their subscription at any time via the "Manage subscription & billing" option in the in-app Staff screen, which opens Stripe's secure billing portal – no need to email us, though you're welcome to if you'd rather.
  • Refunds: fees already paid are non-refundable, including for the remainder of a partial year. Cancelling stops future renewals but doesn't refund the current year.
